A shop the size of one shelf

Curious Mind sells twelve ebooks at a time. Not twelve thousand — twelve. Everything on it was read cover to cover by a person who was willing to argue about it afterwards.

We started in 2024 because browsing had stopped feeling like browsing. Every shop had everything, ranked by an algorithm optimising for something that was not our reading. The fix we wanted was embarrassingly simple: fewer books, chosen by someone whose taste you could learn.

So that is the whole model. Twelve titles. If something goes on the shelf, something comes off. Chapter one of every book is free to read before you decide, because a sample beats a five-star average from strangers. And every file is DRM-free, which means the thing you buy is a file you own, not a licence we can revoke.

Authors keep 70%

Most of what you pay goes to the person who wrote the book. The rest covers hosting, payment fees, and the reading. We are not trying to grow into something that needs a growth team.

Reading is not a local business

About seven in ten of our readers are outside the country we file our taxes in, so the shop is built for that rather than apologising for it: prices display in your own currency, VAT and local tax are worked out from your country at checkout, and every purchase downloads instantly — there is nothing to ship and no customs to clear.

Questions we actually get

What does DRM-free actually mean?

The file has no lock on it. You can copy it to another device, keep a backup, read it in ten years on software that does not exist yet, or lend it to a friend the way you would lend a paperback. We are trusting you, which is generally the cheaper policy.

Can I buy from outside the US or Europe?

Yes — we sell to readers in more than seventy countries. Choose your country at checkout and the price, the currency and the tax adjust. Cards, PayPal and bank transfer are all accepted, and delivery is instant everywhere because there is nothing physical to send.

Why is there tax on an ebook?

Most countries treat digital goods as taxable where the buyer lives rather than where the seller does. We show the rate for your country on the summary before you pay, rather than surprising you with it at the end. If you are an EU business with a valid VAT ID you can enter it and the tax is reversed.

Do I need an account?

No. Your bag, your saved list and your library live in your own browser, and the files are emailed to you. There is no password to forget and no profile for us to lose.

What is the refund policy?

Fourteen days, no questions, even though you already have the files. If a book was not for you, it was not for you. Write to us and it is done the same week.

How do you choose the twelve?

One question: does it still move six months later, without the mood you were in when you found it? Most books do not survive that. The ones that do go on the shelf until something better arrives.
